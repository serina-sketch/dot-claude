# Coach mode — what I want to achieve

The full rules are in `SKILL.md`. This file is the why.

## The goal
Coach mode is for **building something and understanding it**. I'm the one who designs it: what it does, how the pieces fit, the choices that matter. The AI writes the details and does the tedious parts. When it's done, I should be able to explain how and why it works, because I'm the one who decided.

## What usually goes wrong
- I've been letting AI do everything, so I don't know how to make the thing myself. Example: I've made a chatbot, but I still don't know how to write even a basic chatbot character.

## What I want from it
- Know what goes into the thing I'm making. For a chatbot character: what goes into a character card, like personality, setting, and so on.
- I decide the big overall picture. Then the AI fills in the gaps.

## What success looks like
- I know what it takes to make the thing. For a chatbot: how the sections are designed, what belongs where, and what's useful and what isn't.

## Where it came from
A video by Noah Kim about his plugin, VibeWise (transcript in `transcripts/`). His points:
- By default, AI feeds you everything to make it fast and easy. It asks a couple of questions, offers a "recommended" answer, and you click yes, yes, yes.
- With multiple choice, everyone picks A and moves on. Nothing is learned.
- Then it hands you a huge plan you'll never really read. You didn't build anything; you said what you wanted and it did the rest.
- What makes building fun, and what makes you good at it, is the many small decisions: how should this behave, what happens when something gets deleted, how do the pieces connect. AI takes those away when it shouldn't.
- The fix: you don't have to write every line yourself, but you do the thinking. Free answers, not picks. Design first, then the AI makes it.

## How it fits with the other modes
- **Tutor mode:** I do all the thinking and all the doing. The deepest learning, for school and skills I need in my own hands.
- **Coach mode:** I do the thinking, the AI does the doing. I learn judgment and design, and the project still gets finished.
- **Maid mode:** I give the ideas, the AI does the work. The goal is output, not learning.
